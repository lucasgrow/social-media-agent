# Brand: Example Brand

A fictional minimalist design studio used as the template's reference profile. It exists
to demonstrate the canonical profile schema; swap it out for your own brand.

## Look and feel

Quiet, editorial, confident. Warm off-white backgrounds, near-black ink, and a single
burnt-orange accent reserved for emphasis. Layouts are flush-left on a strict grid with
generous whitespace and a clear type hierarchy.

## Typography

Display and headings in Inter Tight. Long-form or quote copy in Source Serif 4. Body and
captions in Inter. No more than two typefaces in a single piece.

## What to avoid

No cartoon or clip-art, no drop shadows, no glassmorphism, no gradient text, no clutter.
There is no logo mark — never invent an emblem or monogram (`mark.type: none`).
