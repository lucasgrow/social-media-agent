# Voice — Example Brand

Plain and unhurried. We say one thing well rather than three things loudly. We avoid hype
words and marketing filler, keep sentences short, and let the design carry the emphasis.

## Contexts

- **announcement** — a single headline, one line of context, a soft call to action.
- **utility** — practical, scannable copy with no jargon.
- **story** — a short hook, one supporting line, and a clean close.
